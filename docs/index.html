    How it is calculated

    Import cost = (LME + CIF premium) × USD/CNY × (1 + duty) × (1 + VAT) + misc costs.  Import P/L = SHFE − import cost; positive means the import window is open.
    Spot compares the SHFE 1st month (连一) with LME cash. 3M compares the SHFE 3rd month (连三) with LME 3-month. Ratio = SHFE ÷ LME; breakeven ratio = import cost ÷ LME.
    SHFE−LME $ = SHFE 1st month with VAT taken out, in USD, minus LME cash. Breakeven premium = the highest CIF premium an importer could pay today and still break even. Cash–3M and SHFE calendar spreads are near minus far: positive = backwardation, negative = contango.
    LME term structure: cash–3M = cash minus 3-month (positive = backwardation, negative = contango); % p.a. = cash–3M ÷ cash × 360 ÷ 91 days. Full carry = cash × SOFR × 91/360 + warehouse rent × 91 days, the most contango that storage and financing can justify; contango near 100% of full carry signals ample nearby metal. The 6-month range and percentile use the last 126 LME days.
    Gold, silver and crude use the most-active China contract (by open interest). Shanghai premium = China price with VAT removed, converted to USD/oz (or USD/bbl), minus the international price. Import P/L = China price − (international + your cost) × USD/CNY × (1 + duty) × (1 + VAT), in CNY/g (gold), CNY/kg (silver). Crude: SC − Brent in USD/bbl; SC is quoted net of duty and VAT. Main − next = most-active minus the next most-active later contract.
    Timing: China prices are settlement prices from the Shanghai close; LBMA gold is the London fixing, and COMEX silver, Brent and WTI are the same-day front-month closes in New York, so part of any daily move is just the time gap.
    Sources: LME official prices and stocks and the LBMA gold price via Westmetall; SHFE and INE settlement prices from shfe.com.cn and ine.cn; COMEX silver, Brent and WTI via Yahoo Finance; USD/CNY = ECB reference rate via frankfurter.dev.
